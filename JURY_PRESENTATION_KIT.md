## 2. 📝 Hack2Skill Submission Form Cheat Sheet

### Project Title
> **ReasonLens: AI-Powered Socratic Thinking Companion powered by Gemini 2.5 Flash & Google Antigravity**

### Problem Statement (2 Clear Sentences)
> High-stakes decisions often fail not from technical incompetence, but from unstated fragile assumptions and unexamined confirmation bias. Conventional LLMs exacerbate this by acting as compliant "yes-men" or attempting to usurp human agency by dictating what to do.

### Solution Overview (1-2 Paragraphs)
> **ReasonLens** is an intelligent Socratic companion designed to stress-test human reasoning without making decisions for the user. By isolating Decision Context from Proposed Rationale, ReasonLens leverages Gemini 2.5 Flash with structured JSON schemas to extract unmasked assumptions, assign vulnerability ratings (`HIGH`, `MED`, `LOW`), simulate prospective 12-month pre-mortem failure scenarios, and formulate piercing Socratic inquiries that challenge blind spots.
> 
> Built in Google Antigravity following Clean Architecture, ReasonLens enforces strict modular separation (<150 lines per file), frosted glassmorphism, tactile keyboard ergonomics (`Ctrl+Enter`), and a defensive dual-mode architecture that guarantees zero-downtime presentations through local mock fallbacks.

### Prompt Strategy & Antigravity Narrative (Judges Love This!)
> *"Our development methodology centered on a 4-tier strategic filter executed autonomously in Google Antigravity:
> 1. **Strategic Problem Deconstruction:** Discarded non-software hurdles and isolated cognitive friction into data, assumption vulnerability, and pre-mortem simulation.
> 2. **Socratic System Framing:** Enforced a strict behavioral invariant on Gemini 2.5 Flash: never recommend a path or declare a winner; exclusively interrogate premises, surface unstated axioms, and simulate catastrophic failure catalysts.
> 3. **Deterministic Schema Enforcement:** Enforced Gemini `responseSchema` for type-safe structured extraction matching our TypeScript domain interfaces.
> 4. **Defensive High Availability:** Integrated client-side streaming token buffers and an instant fail-safe switch to maintain 100% availability during evaluation."*
